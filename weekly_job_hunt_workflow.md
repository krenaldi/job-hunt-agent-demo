# Weekly Job Hunt Workflow

Follow these steps each week, in order.

1. **Load context**: read `job_search_profile.md` and `job_leads.md`.
2. **Search**: look for new openings matching the profile (job boards, company career pages). TODO: list preferred sources.
3. **Filter**: discard anything that breaks a constraint in the profile. Note why for borderline cases.
4. **Dedupe**: skip roles already in `job_leads.md` (match on company + role).
5. **Record**: append new leads to `job_leads.md` with link, date found, fit notes, and status `New`.
6. **Follow up**: list leads with status `Applied` or `Interviewing` that have had no update in 7+ days.
7. **Summarize**: report top new leads (best fit first), follow-ups due, and suggested next actions.

## Rules
- Never apply or send messages without the user's explicit approval; draft only.
- Don't claim experience that isn't in the resume or profile.
